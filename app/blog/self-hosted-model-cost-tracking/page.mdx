If you run agents against your own Ollama, vLLM, or gateway endpoints, you can
now give those models a price and have Omnigent track what they cost. Set the
per-token rates once in your provider config and every session on that provider
reports spend, just like the vendor-hosted models Omnigent already knows.

Until now, a model that wasn't in the pricing catalog showed no cost, so
self-hosted runs were a blind spot and your spend caps couldn't see them.
Configured pricing fills that gap: you tell Omnigent the input and output rates
your endpoint charges (or the internal rate you want to attribute), and it uses
those to compute session cost. Configured rates take precedence over the
catalog, so a gateway serving a known model id at your own price is metered at
your price. Because this feeds the same usage totals as everything else, cost
budget policies now hold for self-hosted models too.

Add a `pricing` block to the provider family in your provider config with the
per-million-token rates for input and output. Cache rates are optional and
default from the input rate when omitted.

```yaml
providers:
  ollama-local:
    kind: local
    openai:
      base_url: http://localhost:11434/v1
      api_key: ollama
      pricing:
        input_per_million: 0.25
        output_per_million: 1.0
```

See [Models & Credentials](/docs/build/models) for how providers and
credentials are configured, and [built-in policies](/docs/policies/builtin) for
the `cost_budget` policy that these prices now feed.
