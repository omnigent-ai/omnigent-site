If you own a shared session and cannot always be the one to clear an agent's
privileged actions, you can now hand that authority to a collaborator you
trust. The agent keeps working while a teammate approves the prompts you used
to have to catch yourself.

Sharing a session with someone as an editor lets them steer the run, but until
now only the owner could approve a privileged action like a `git push`, so the
agent stalled whenever you stepped away. You can now grant a specific editor
the right to approve those actions too. Everyone else stays where they were: an
ordinary editor can reject a pending action but cannot approve it, and approval
authority is a separate switch you control per person, never a transfer of
ownership.

To delegate it, open the session's Share dialog, find the collaborator, and turn
on their approval authority. The switch is only available once they have at
least edit access, and it never applies to public link access. Only the owner
can change it, and you can turn it back off at any time. For how session sharing
and access levels work, see the
[collaboration guide](/docs/collaborate/auth#session-sharing).
