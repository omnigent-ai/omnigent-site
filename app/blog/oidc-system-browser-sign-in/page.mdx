If your team runs Omnigent behind company single sign-on, the desktop app now
signs you in through your normal browser. Google sign-in, passkeys, your
password manager, and the identity-provider session you already have all just
work, and the app stays signed in across relaunches.

Identity providers were a poor fit inside an app window: Google refuses
embedded browsers, and passkeys and password managers have nothing to attach
to. Now, when you select Connect, the app opens your system browser at the
server's sign-in page. Once you finish, the tab says "Return to Omnigent" and
the app comes to the front, signed in. The identity provider never appears
inside the app. From then on, relaunching, opening a new window, following a
deep link, or switching servers reuses your session or renews it quietly in
the background. The browser only opens again when nothing can renew the
session, and the connect screen tells you why (expired, ended by the server,
or sign-in required). Your server's access rules still decide who gets in: a
sign-in from a domain it does not allow is refused, and signing out from
Settings ends the session for every window on that server.

There is nothing to set up on the desktop side. Open **Server → Change
Server…**, enter your server URL, and select **Connect**. Servers that use
built-in accounts, header auth, or Databricks sign in exactly as before. On the
server, this applies to any deployment with `OMNIGENT_AUTH_PROVIDER=oidc`, and
this release also lets operators register Omnigent with their identity provider
as a public client (no client secret) and point it at explicit authorization,
token, and signing-key endpoints when discovery is unavailable:

```bash
OMNIGENT_AUTH_PROVIDER=oidc
OMNIGENT_OIDC_ISSUER=https://identity.example.com
OMNIGENT_OIDC_CLIENT_ID=omnigent
# Public client: opt in and leave OMNIGENT_OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET unset
OMNIGENT_OIDC_TOKEN_ENDPOINT_AUTH_METHOD=none
```

See [Auth & SSO](/docs/collaborate/auth#single-sign-on-oidc) for the full
server configuration and the [desktop app guide](/docs/interact/desktop) for
connecting to a server.
