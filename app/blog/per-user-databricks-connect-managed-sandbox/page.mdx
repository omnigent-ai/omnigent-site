If you run agents in a managed Omnigent sandbox and your work touches data in
Databricks, you can now connect your own Databricks workspace and have those
agents reach it as you.

Until now, a sandbox that talked to Databricks leaned on a shared or static
token, so everyone's agents acted as the same account and rotating or revoking
access was a manual chore. Now you authorize your workspace once, the same way
you connect GitHub, and the server mints a short-lived token for you on demand
and refreshes it when it expires. The raw token never lands on the sandbox
disk, and each stored credential stays encrypted at rest and bound to the
identity that owns it, so an agent's access to Databricks is your access.

Open Settings, find Integrations, and choose to connect Databricks. Enter your
workspace URL, complete the sign-in in your workspace, and you are connected.
From then on, the agents you run in the sandbox reach your Databricks workspace
with your identity, and you can disconnect from the same place at any time. For
how these per-user credentials are stored and protected, see the
[credential store docs](/docs/deploy/credential-store).
