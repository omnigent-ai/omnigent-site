If you ask your agents to show that a fix really works, you can now watch the
proof without leaving the conversation. When an agent saves a screen recording
in its workspace and links it in a reply, that reply shows a compact video
player you can start with one click.

Until now, checking an agent's recording meant finding the file, downloading
it, and opening it somewhere else. Now you press Play in the chat, then pause,
seek, or go fullscreen with the usual browser controls. Click the recording's
title to open a larger preview in the file viewer. If the agent also saves a
chapter file next to the video, that preview lists each step the agent took.
Click a step to jump to that moment, and the list highlights the current step
as the video plays. Nothing loads until you press Play, so a long session full
of recordings stays fast. The same player works on desktop and mobile.

To use it, have the agent save a video file (such as `.webm` or `.mp4`) in the
workspace and reply with a Markdown link to it, like
`[Screen recording](recordings/verification.webm)`. For clickable steps, it
saves a standard WebVTT chapter file named after the full video filename with
`.chapters.vtt` appended:

```vtt
WEBVTT

open
00:00.000 --> 00:04.500
Open the application

submit
00:04.500 --> 00:09.000
Submit the form

verify
00:09.000 --> 00:15.000
Confirmation appears
```

Save that as `recordings/verification.webm.chapters.vtt` and the steps appear
when you open the recording's preview. Without a chapter file, the recording
still plays normally. The
[recordings guide](https://github.com/omnigent-ai/omnigent/blob/main/docs/video-recordings.md)
covers the chapter format, its limits, and how remote video links behave.
